What the drawings do—and do not—establish

These are original SVG compositions, not crops or traces of the copyrighted illustration. The green/tee symbols and fairway/tree/water edges are deliberately schematic. No exact tee color position, bunker, carry distance, landing-zone marker or measured width is claimed. A stylistic approval does not verify any unresolved geography.

Hole 5

Map-supported: Short tee-to-green route between the areas used for holes 4 and 6; Green at the end of the route; Trees surrounding the corridor.

Unresolved: Exact position of each colored tee; Fairway and green boundary dimensions; Bunker identities and edges; Elevation, carry distances and preferred landing zone.

Hole 12

Map-supported: A direct diagonal connection from the tee area toward a green southwest of it on the north-up municipal map; Tree cover around the corridor.

Unresolved: Exact tee and green positions for each tee set; Precise fairway width; Bunker edges; Elevation and carry distances.

Hole 15

Map-supported: Longer route on the eastern side of the published course map; Green at the southern end; Water near the approach corridor; Tree cover around the hole.

Unresolved: Exact water edge and its distance from the playing line; Fairway and green contours; Tee-set locations; Bunker edges; Carry distances and safest target zone.
